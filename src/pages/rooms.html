Worth the trip on their own

The Angel & Cranberry Baths

We take the Victorian experience seriously, and the shared bathrooms are no exception. There is also a public powder room on the main floor.

The Angel Bath

A claw-foot tub with an antique telephone shower, hand-painted cherubs and clouds across the sixteen-foot ceiling, and a ceiling fan for the warm months. It is the most photographed room in the house, and we understand why.

The Cranberry Bath

For those who prefer a quick rinse, the Cranberry Bath has a convenient walk-in shower — the right spot before a day out in Beaver, or after a long one.
