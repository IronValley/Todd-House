Welcome

A living piece of Beaver's rich past

Step into a slice of history at The Todd House, a fully restored French Second Empire Victorian gem built in 1873. We're not just another place to rest your head — we're a living, breathing piece of Beaver's past, recognized by the Historic Beaver Association for our meticulous restoration.

With 37 years of innkeeping behind us, we've perfected the art of blending old-world charm with modern comfort. As a local, family-owned establishment, we take immense pride in our quality of service and our commitment to this community.

Everything we serve is sourced locally in Beaver, giving you a true taste of our town. From the moment you walk through our doors, you'll be wrapped in the warmth of our hospitality and the comfort of our spacious, well-appointed bedrooms.

— Jim & Linda Todd, your hosts

The morning ritual

Breakfast, and the table it's served on

Every morning begins with a home-cooked breakfast, traditional and American, prepared with ingredients sourced from local Beaver purveyors and served on formal china beneath the dining-room chandelier.

There is no dress code. Come as you are, sit with the other guests, and let the day start slowly.

  • Locally sourced ingredients from our Beaver neighbors
  • Served in the historic dining room, seating up to ten
  • Dietary needs gladly accommodated — just let us know
The common rooms

The whole house is yours

A parlor and antiquarian library, a music room with a pump organ and player piano, a butler's pantry of Victorian curiosities, a game room, a walled courtyard and a wraparound porch.

Parlor & Library

The Music Room

The region

Your gateway to Beaver

We sit in the heart of downtown Beaver, at the confluence of two rivers. Restaurants, shops and the Merrick Art Gallery are an easy walk from the front porch, and the parks are a short stroll further.
